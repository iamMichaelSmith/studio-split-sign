## Publishing Notes

- Publish one foundational post first, then interlink every later article back to the split sheet guide.
- Use plain-language examples from real studio scenarios: beat maker plus featured artist, remote topliner, producer plus songwriter, and engineer present in the room.
- Add downloadable lead magnets later: split sheet checklist, sync readiness checklist, and studio closeout checklist.
- Every article should include a product CTA above the final third of the post and again at the end.
- Build internal links around `/blog`, `/pricing`, `/split-sheet`, and the VST plugin section on the landing page.

## Source Anchors

- U.S. Copyright Office: musicians need to understand that a musical work and a sound recording are separate copyright-protected works.
- U.S. Copyright Office: registration creates a public ownership record and may provide additional enforcement benefits.
- Songtrust: split sheets should include legal names, contact information, ownership percentages, affiliations, metadata, signatures, and execution dates.
- The MLC: co-writers who can register and collect their shares should register their shares, and MLC activity does not replace other registration activities.
