Session closeout checklist

Record what is agreed, what is ready, and who follows up next. Use a five-minute timebox when the terms are already settled.

Closeout contact:

  1. Confirm the people. Record legal names, current emails, roles, and exact credit spelling. Separate songwriting ownership from performance and production credits.
  2. Record the agreed composition shares. Check the 100% total and let every writer review the whole record. If disputed, keep it a draft; do not pressure anyone to sign.
  3. Flag unknown rights details. Note publishers, administrators, performance rights organizations (PROs), writer/publisher identifiers (IPIs), master rights, producer terms, and third-party licenses. Assign unknowns to a person and a date.
  4. Record signature status. Send the agreed version for review. Keep pending signatures distinguishable from a completed agreement. Share the completed PDF after signing finishes.
  5. Confirm the delivery. List the exact audio versions, file locations, recipients, and approval status. A review bounce is not a statement that release or licensing permissions are complete.
  6. Send the recap. Include completed items, open questions, follow-up owners, deadlines, and where the current documents are stored.
